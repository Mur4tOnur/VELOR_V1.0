## Product Context

VELOR is a market intelligence platform built for investors and traders who want to manage everything in one place.

Core value:
- Bring market context, portfolio visibility, and signal clarity into one platform
- Help users identify expensive vs discounted conditions
- Help users monitor multiple brokers and accounts from one dashboard
- Reduce noise, emotional decisions, and fragmented workflows

VELOR is NOT a financial advisor.
VELOR does NOT promise profits.
VELOR helps users detect market conditions and organize their decision-making.

Signal language:
- FADE = expensive / stretched condition
- OVERHEAT = extreme expensive / overheated condition
- DISCOUNT = discounted / attractive condition
- VALUE = deep value condition

Core dashboard areas:
- Indicator Engine
- Fear & Greed
- Smart Alerts
- Portfolio Risk
- Multi-broker account visibility
- Liquidation Heat Map
- Funding / ETF / market context panels

Tone:
- Premium
- Clear

- Fintech
- Confident but not hype
- Not overly technical
- Avoid cringe crypto marketing language
